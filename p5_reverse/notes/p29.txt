1 답 ⟦8over3⟧
⟦ int _{0} ^{2} {x^2 dx}& = LEFT [ 1 over 3  x^3  RIGHT ]  _{0} ^{2} #& = 1 over 3 times 2^3 -0= 8 over 3 ⟧
2 답 ⑴ ⟦3⟧		⑵ ⟦- 1over3⟧
⑴ ⟦ int _{1} ^{2} {2x`dx} = LEFT [ x  ^{2} RIGHT ]  _{1} ^{2}  = 2^2 -1^2 =3⟧
⑵ ⟦ int _{0} ^{1} {(-x  ^{2} )dx} &= LEFT [- 1 over 3  x^3  RIGHT ]  _{0} ^{1}  #&= - 1 over 3 times 1^3 -0= - 1 over 3 ⟧
3 답 ⟦32⟧  
⟦int_1^2 (6x^2 +12x)dx= LEFT [ 2x  ^{3} +6x^2  RIGHT ]  _{1} ^{2}⟧
⟦=(16+24)-(2+6)⟧⟦=32⟧
4 답 ⟦12⟧
⟦ int _{-1} ^{2} {(3x  ^{2} +1)dx} = LEFT [ x  ^{3} +x RIGHT ]  _{-1} ^{2}⟧
              ⟦=(8+2)-(-1-1)⟧⟦=12⟧
5 답 ⑴ ⟦-6⟧		⑵ ⟦- 7over2⟧
⑴ ⟦int_-2 ^1⟧⟦(2x-1)dx⟧
⟦= LEFT [ x  ^{2} -x RIGHT ]  _{-2} ^{1}⟧
⟦=(1-1)-(4+2)=-6⟧	
⑵ ⟦int_1 ^2⟧⟦(2x^3 -6x^2 +2x) dx⟧
⟦= LEFT [ 1 over 2  x  ^{4} -2x^3 + x^2  RIGHT ]  _{1} ^{2}⟧
⟦=(8-16+4) - left (1 over 2 -2 +1 right )  =  - 7 over 2 ⟧
6 답 ⑴ ⟦-2⟧		⑵ ⟦-17⟧
⑴ ⟦int_1 ^-1⟧⟦(3x^2 -4x) dx⟧=⟦- int_-1 ^1⟧⟦(3x^2 -4x) dx⟧
⟦= -LEFT [ x  ^{3} -2x^2  RIGHT ]  _{-1} ^{1}⟧
⟦=-left {(1-2)-(-1-2) right }=-2⟧
⑵ ⟦int_2 ^1⟧⟦(4x^3 +2) dx⟧⟦= LEFT [ x  ^{4} +2x RIGHT ]  _{2} ^{1}⟧
⟦=(1+2)-(16+4)=-17⟧
7 답 ⟦6⟧
⟦int_-2^1 {(x^2 +4x+3)}dx ⟧⟦= LEFT [ 1 over 3  x  ^{3} +2x^2 +3x RIGHT ]  _{-2} ^{1}⟧
⟦=  left (1  over 3 +2+3 right ) - left (  - 8 over 3 +8 -6 right )⟧⟦ = 16 over 3  - left ( - 2  over 3 right ) =6⟧
8 답 ⟦- 1over3⟧
⟦int _{ 0} ^{ 2} { LEFT ( x^2 +2kx + 1over3 RIGHT ) `dx} `=` LEFT [ 1 over 3 x^3 + kx^2 + 1 over 3 x RIGHT ]_0 ^2⟧
                   ⟦= 10 over 3 +4k⟧
따라서 ⟦10 over 3 +4k=2⟧이므로 ⟦ k = - 1 over 3⟧
9 답 ⑴ ⟦1 over 4⟧   		⑵ ⟦-  8 over 3⟧
⑴ ⟦int_0^1 {x^3} dx⟧⟦= LEFT [  1 over 4 x^4 RIGHT ]  _{0} ^{1} = 1 over 4 times 1 ^4 -0= 1 over 4⟧
⑵ ⟦ int _{0} ^{2} {(-x^2 )dx} &= LEFT [ - 1 over 3  x^3  RIGHT ]  _{0} ^{2} #& = - 1 over 3 times 2^3 -0=  - 8 over 3 ⟧
10 답 ⑴ ⟦9⟧   ⑵ ⟦64 over 3⟧   ⑶ ⟦- 3 over 2⟧   ⑷ ⟦-4⟧
⑴ ⟦int_0^3 {2x}dx⟧⟦= LEFT [   x^2  RIGHT ]  _{0} ^{3} =  3^2 -0=9⟧	
⑵ ⟦int_0^4 {x^2 }dx⟧⟦= LEFT [  1 over 3 x^3  RIGHT ]  _{0} ^{4} = 1 over 3 times 4^3 -0= 64 over 3⟧
⑶ ⟦int_0^1 {(-3x)}dx⟧⟦= LEFT [  - 3 over 2 x^2  RIGHT ]  _{0} ^{1} = - 3  over 2  times 1^2 -0=  - 3 over 2 ⟧
